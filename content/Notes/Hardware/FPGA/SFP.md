Ethernet cables use a **RJ45** connector plug into OSI model layer 1-2 devices.

Switch typically only has SFP port. So you want to use an SFP connector to convert RJ45 connector to bridge the cable to the device.

Ethernet cables like (Cat5e, Cat6) uses copper which is typically only used for short distance, like within your home.

Fiber optic is used for long distance. 

So most switches are designed to accept SFP port because they are modular.

We have RJ45 SFP module which convert ethernet to SFP
We have Fiber SFP module which convert fiber optic to SFP

SFP
100 Mb - Ethernet only
1000 Mb (1Gb) - Fiber/ Ethernet

SFP+
10 Gb over fiber, ethernet, DAC
