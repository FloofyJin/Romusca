Different types are thick film resistor, thin film resistor, wirewound resistors, carbon film resistors, metal oxide resistors, varistors, magneto resistors. thermistors, light dependent resistors (LDRs), and fusible resistors

### Thick film resistor
Cheap. Offers higher power rating up to 2W. Exhibit a higher temperature coefficient of around +- 100ppm/C meaning their resistance varies more with temperature changes.

### Think film resistor
More expensive. Characterized by highly accurate resistive layer of vapor on insulating surface. Typically made up of glass or ceramic. excellent stability over time and temperature with low temperature coefficient.

### Wirewound resistors
Made by winding a metal wire, usually nickel-chromin or tungsten, around a ceramic or fiberglass core. Can operate under extreme temperatures without significant change in resistance.

### Carbon film resistors
Most common. Produced by depositing a layer of carbon upon a ceramic substrate. Can usually handle power ratings of 0.125W to 2W. Temperature coefficient of +-200ppm/C
