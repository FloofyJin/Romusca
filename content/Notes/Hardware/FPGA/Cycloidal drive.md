type of gear reduction mechanism that converts "high-speed low-torque" rotation from a motor into "low-speed high-torque rotation".

It commonly has more pins  then there are lobes on the cycloidal disc. Typical design has one more pin.

Reduction ratio = (Number of housing pins - 1) / 1

eg.
- 20 pins → 19:1
- 60 pins → 59:1
- 101 pins → 100:1
