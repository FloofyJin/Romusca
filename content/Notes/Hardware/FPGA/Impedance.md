Impedance is resistance in a circuit characterized by total opposition to AC current. impedance in circuit can be affected by inductor and capacitance. impedance due to capacitor goes down as frequency increase. Impedance due to inductor increases as frequency increases. 

There are no impedance in a DC circuit. At high frequency, low impedance allows more current to flow through. In that case, you want to use capacitor to smooth out the voltage while also minimizing impedance.

Cap in high frequency is used for filter and decoupling

Inductors in high frequency is useful for limiting high frequency of currents.
